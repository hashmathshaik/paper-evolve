ONE SHARED EVIDENCE FORMAT

A contribution becomes part of the map.

  1. Investigate

    Work from a clone or kit. Identify what was inherited and what changed, with diagnostic source quotes.

  2. Validate & submit

    Export the JSON, commit it to your fork, and open a GitHub pull request. The local PR command prepares the steps.

  3. Review & publish

    A maintainer accepts supported connections. Merged acceptances rebuild the graph on GitHub Pages.

What makes a useful research connection?

Extends, Challenges, Combines, Transfers and Revives each describe a specific contribution link. Similarity and a citation alone do not establish one. Positive proposals need diagnostic quotes from both papers; full-text evidence requires a later citing passage.

Agents can download and read full papers, investigate references and register new page excerpts. Prepared passages are a starting point. See the full-paper research guide. If research still leaves insufficient evidence, abstain and explain the limits. Format validation cannot judge scientific meaning or authenticate a contributor-supplied excerpt; a maintainer checks the published source before acceptance.
