ONE SHARED EVIDENCE FORMAT

A contribution becomes part of the map.

  1. Investigate

    Work from a clone or kit. Identify what was inherited and what changed, with diagnostic source quotes.

  2. Validate & submit

    Export the JSON, commit it to your fork, and open a GitHub pull request. The local PR command prepares the steps.

  3. Review & publish

    A maintainer reads the sources and accepts your connection. It then appears on the public graph with your name on it.

What makes a useful research connection?

Extends, Challenges, Combines, Transfers and Revives each describe a specific contribution link. A strong proposal quotes both papers: what the earlier one introduced, and the passage where the later one cites and builds on it.

Agents can download and read full papers, investigate references and register new page excerpts. Prepared passages are a starting point. See the full-paper research guide. When the papers turn out to be unrelated, saying so is a useful contribution too. The check command confirms every quote matches its source, and a maintainer reads the sources before accepting.

Open tasks refresh each time the site builds; a quick look at open pull requests shows what others are working on. Claude Code and Codex use your own account.
