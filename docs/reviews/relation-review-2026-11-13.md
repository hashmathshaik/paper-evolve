# Relation evidence review — 2026-11-13

This is an assistant review of source evidence, not independent human expert ground truth. I read the two supplied abstracts and available citing sentences before inspecting the Haiku label and rationale. Primary source pages were checked for disputed identities and missed derivations where accessible. This is not a full-paper review of every case.

The full review covers 34 pairs: the 30-pair sample below plus four additional Jev-negative audits.

The frozen snapshot contained 2,497 completed comparisons and 679 accepted pairs. Seed 20261113 selected 16 random accepted pairs, four additional Challenges pairs, four additional Transfers pairs, and six random rejected pairs. Targeted strata must not be combined into a corpus-wide accuracy estimate.

## Findings

- Random accepted: 14/16 labels supportable from the supplied evidence (one has an overstated evidence basis); two need source-specific context. This is a small assistant-reviewed sample, not established 87.5% accuracy.
- Targeted Challenges: three supportable, one wrong type. One supportable label requires distinguishing a disputed interpretation from an agreed observation.
- Targeted Transfers: three supportable, one wrong type.
- Rejections: three reasonable abstentions, one uncertain case, two likely missed relationships.
- Combines and Revives had no accepted predictions in the snapshot, so their quality is unmeasured.
- The model often outputs exactly 0.85; this score must not be treated as calibrated accuracy. Raising the threshold alone does not fix semantic errors.

## Changes made

Four audited accepted edges are withheld rather than silently relabeled. One evidence-basis claim is downgraded to inference. The two missed candidates are recorded for targeted follow-up; they are not automatically promoted. All original responses are preserved. Future prompts clarify shared-failure versus criticism, within-task improvements versus transfer, and attribution to the specific earlier paper. The prompt changes have not been validated on a held-out sample.
